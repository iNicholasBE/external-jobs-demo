Push, not poll

  1. You submit a prompt — JobRunr creates an External Job
  2. The trigger calls Veo's :predictLongRunning and stores the operation name
  3. The External Job parks in PROCESSED
  4. When the video is ready, Google POSTs video.generated to this app
  5. We verify the signature, fetch the MP4, and signal the External Job

Compare to /gpu, which polls Replicate every 5 seconds — same External Jobs API, opposite direction of integration.
